Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026 · Plain-language explanation of how PeachyPrint handles your files and data.

1. Local-Only Image Processing (Zero File Uploads)

PeachyPrint is built with privacy at its core. When you drop, paste, or select an image in our paper-format and print-resolution simulator, that file is decoded and rendered entirely inside your own browser using standard HTML5 FileReader and Canvas APIs.

We do not upload, transmit, store, inspect, or share your images or exported PNG, JPEG, or PDF files on any server. Once you close or refresh your browser tab, your artwork is cleared from browser memory automatically.

2. Information You Voluntarily Provide

If you submit a message through our Contact page, we receive the name, email address, topic, and message text you choose to send solely so we can respond to your inquiry or improve PeachyPrint. We never sell or rent your contact information to third parties.

3. Advertising & Google AdSense Cookies

To keep PeachyPrint free for everyone, this website uses Google AdSense (publisher ID ca-pub-4588627708948117), an advertising service provided by Google LLC.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to PeachyPrint and/or other sites on the internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you may opt out of third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Standard Server Log Files

Like most web hosting platforms (such as Render), our server infrastructure may automatically record basic technical request logs (such as anonymized IP address, browser type, requested URL path, HTTP status code, and timestamp) to maintain uptime, prevent abuse, and diagnose technical errors. These logs are not linked to any uploaded artwork.

5. Managing Cookies in Your Browser

You can disable or clear cookies at any time through your browser’s privacy settings. Disabling third-party cookies will not break PeachyPrint’s interactive paper simulator or PNG/PDF export functionality.

6. Children’s Privacy

PeachyPrint is a general-audience design utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information via our contact form, please contact us so we can delete it promptly.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your region (including the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and California), you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data you voluntarily sent us, as well as the right to opt out of personalized advertising cookies.

8. Updates & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically as our features or legal requirements evolve. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. For any privacy questions, please reach out via our Contact page.
