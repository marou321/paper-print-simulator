✦ About PeachyPrint

Making Print Sizing Visual, Friendly & Foolproof

No more guessing whether your artwork will look crisp on A2 or turn blocky at the copy shop. PeachyPrint turns print DPI math into an intuitive pastel paper studio.

Why We Built PeachyPrint

Most people only discover that an image is too low-resolution after paying for a poster or picking up a stack of flyers. Traditional DPI calculators give you abstract numbers in a spreadsheet, without letting you actually see how an A4 sheet sits inside an A2 poster or what happens the moment you scale it up.

PeachyPrint bridges that gap with real-time visual simulation: you see true ISO 216 paper proportions side-by-side, drag and resize directly on the target canvas, get immediate Green-to-Red border feedback as pixel density changes, and export print-ready PNG, JPEG, or true physical-size PDF files in one click.

How Our Print Resolution Math Works

PeachyPrint reads the exact native pixel dimensions (width × height) of your uploaded image or sample sheet. As you choose a source format (A5–A0), place it on a target format, and scale it up or down, we compute the physical printed dimensions in millimeters and inches (inches = mm ÷ 25.4) and calculate:

Effective DPI = min( Image Width ÷ Printed Width in Inches, Image Height ÷ Printed Height in Inches )
  • Green Border (Sharp / Safe): Effective DPI meets or exceeds your chosen print profile (150+ DPI for standard prints, 240+ DPI for fine art/photos, or 100+ DPI for wall posters).
  • Red Border (Pixelation Warning): Effective DPI drops below the safe threshold, indicating noticeable softness, jagged edges, or blockiness on physical paper.

100% Privacy-First, Local Browser Processing

Your illustrations, client posters, family photos, and documents are never uploaded to a remote server. Everything — reading your image, simulating pixel density, rendering the print magnifier, and generating your PNG or ISO PDF export — happens completely locally in your web browser.
